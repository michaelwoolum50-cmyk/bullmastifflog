# BullmastiffLog: Dog Journal

**The field journal built exclusively for Bullmastiff owners.**

The Bullmastiff was bred for one job: the gamekeeper's night watch. Calm. Fearless. Loyal. A dog like that deserves a record as serious as he is.

BullmastiffLog keeps the daily care, the bloodline, and the training in one private book on your Android device:

- **Guard Dossier** — pedigrees, sire/dam lineage, 100-130+ lb muscle development, skull width, and jet-black mask markings.
- **Night Patrol & Vigil Logs** — estate perimeter sweeps, night watch hours, timberline surveillance, and silent alert observations.
- **Gamekeeper Lore & History** — the 19th-century origins of the Gamekeeper's Night Dog and the famous 60% Mastiff / 40% Bulldog formula.
- **Drool & Slobber Care** — post-patrol hydration, moderate flews hygiene, and towel station routines.
- **100% Offline & Private** — no signal needed in remote woods, rural pastures, or estate grounds. All data and photos stay on your device.
- **Second-Chance Confirmations** — protective prompts prevent accidental record deletions.
- **Complete Data Portability** — export full JSON backups anytime with a single tap.
